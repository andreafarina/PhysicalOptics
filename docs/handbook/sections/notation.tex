\section{Wave Vectors}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Symbol & Meaning & Unit\\
\midrule
$(\kx,\ky)$ & Wave-vector components & rad\,m$^{-1}$\\
$\kvec$         & Wave vector            & rad\,m$^{-1}$\\
$k$             & Wave number            & rad\,m$^{-1}$\\
$\lambda$       & Wavelength             & m\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The spatial frequency and wave vector are related by

\[
\boxed{\kvec = 2\pi\fvec}
\]

or, component-wise,

\[
k_x=2\pi f_x,\qquad
k_y=2\pi f_y.
\]

\section{Functions}

Lower-case letters denote functions in the spatial domain, whereas the corresponding upper-case letters denote their Fourier transforms.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cc}
\toprule
Spatial domain & Fourier domain\\
\midrule
$u(x,y)$ & $U(\fx,\fy)$\\
$g(x,y)$ & $G(\fx,\fy)$\\
$h(x,y)$ & $H(\fx,\fy)$\\
$p(x,y)$ & $P(\fx,\fy)$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
